Shortcuts is Apple's automation app for iPhone and Mac, and it is one of my favorite places to tinker. I build little utilities and toolkits with it, then publish the ones worth sharing on RoutineHub.

## What I build

My main project is SCSettings, a settings toolkit that tucks a pile of system toggles and shortcuts behind one menu. It was inspired by SBSettings, an old jailbreak tweak that did the same thing back when you had to jailbreak to get it. Alongside that I have a handful of smaller shortcuts, plus experiments that never quite made it out of the drafts folder.

If you want to follow along, I have written about some of this on the blog, from an [early look at UI actions](/blog/ui-actions-beta/) to a [live review session on the RoutineHub podcast](/blog/routinehub-podcast-live-review-sessions-5/). You can find everything I have published on my RoutineHub profile.
